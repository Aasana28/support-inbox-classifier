### Policy Overview
Brightwave Digital issues tax invoices on the 1st of every calendar month for ongoing retainer services and milestone-based ad spend management. All invoices are denominated in Indian Rupees (INR) and include applicable 18% GST (GSTIN: 24AAACB1234F1Z6). Payment terms are strictly net 15 days from the date of invoice issuance.

### Approved Tone
Professional, clear, reassuring, and precise regarding figures, dates, and bank details.

### Do and Don't Rules
- DO verify invoice reference numbers (format: BWD-INV-YYYY-XXXX) before answering payment status inquiries.
- DO provide HDFC Bank RTGS/NEFT payment coordinates and UPI ID (brightwave@hdfcbank) for electronic settlement.
- DO state that TDS certificates (Form 16A) must be submitted quarterly to accounts@brightwavedigital.in.
- DON'T offer ad-hoc fee discounts or alter payment due dates without written authorization from the Finance Director.
- DON'T discuss disputed line items over unverified telephone calls; insist on written audit trails.
