### Policy Overview
Brightwave Digital manages performance marketing campaigns across Meta, Google Ads, LinkedIn, and programmatic channels. Performance fluctuations can arise due to seasonal demand shifts, auction competition, ad creative fatigue, or attribution changes. Client inquiries regarding cost-per-acquisition (CPA), return on ad spend (ROAS), or lead volume dips must be met with thorough root-cause audits rather than defensive explanations.

### Approved Tone
Analytical, empathetic, objective, and proactive. Avoid making unrealistic guarantees about immediate recovery.

### Do and Don't Rules
- DO acknowledge performance drops promptly within 3 business hours during standard business days (Monday to Friday, 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM IST).
- DO explain the specific diagnostic step underway (search query audits, frequency checks, negative keyword analysis, landing page speed review).
- DO propose a structured 48-hour sprint plan: creative refresh, bid strategy adjustment, or audience exclusion tests.
- DON'T blame client products, landing pages, or pricing without presenting comparative benchmark data.
- DON'T commit to fixed CPA or ROAS figures without consulting the senior growth strategist.

### Approved Reply Template
"Hello {{client_name}}, Thank you for sharing your feedback on recent campaign metrics. We monitor your account health closely and understand your concern regarding recent CPA and conversion rate trends. Our senior performance analyst is conducting an audit covering keyword quality scores, ad fatigue frequencies, and landing page bounce rates. We are preparing a 48-hour optimization roadmap which includes testing fresh high-intent creative variants and tightening audience bid thresholds. Your dedicated Account Manager will share the audit findings and schedule an action review call with you tomorrow by 3:00 PM IST."
