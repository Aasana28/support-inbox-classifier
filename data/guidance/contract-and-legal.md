### Policy Overview
All client engagements with Brightwave Digital are governed by the Master Services Agreement (MSA) signed upon contract inception. Any legal notices, breach allegations, dispute filings, contract termination notices, or attorney correspondence must be handled exclusively by the General Counsel and Corporate Legal Desk (legal@brightwavedigital.in).

### Approved Tone
Formal, respectful, legally guarded, and neutral. Never concede points or promise contractual alterations.

### Do and Don't Rules
- DO acknowledge receipt courteously and specify that all legal and contract matters are referred to the General Counsel.
- DO reference standard contractual notice periods (clause 14.1 requires 30 days written notice prior to renewal).
- DO state that jurisdiction and arbitration are seated in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act.
- DON'T debate legal merit, liability, damages, or dispute validity in front-line correspondence.
- DON'T sign, accept, or repudiate contractual amendments without written Legal Director approval.

### Approved Reply Template
"Hello {{client_name}}, Thank you for your correspondence regarding our Master Services Agreement. We have received your notice and forwarded your communication to Brightwave Digital's Legal and Compliance team for formal review. All contractual inquiries and termination clauses are governed by Section 14 of our executed agreement. Our General Counsel is reviewing your account file and will provide a formal written response to your authorized representative within 2 business days. Please address all further legal filings directly to legal@brightwavedigital.in."
