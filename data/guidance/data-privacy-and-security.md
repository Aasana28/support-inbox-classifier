### Policy Overview
Brightwave Digital complies with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDP) and GDPR standards. We process client customer data strictly as a data processor under strict confidentiality and encryption protocols (AES-256). Any report of data deletion, subject access requests (SAR), unauthorized access, potential data leaks, or security compromises must trigger an immediate security freeze and executive escalation (SEC-PROTOCOL-1).

### Approved Tone
Utmost seriousness, rigorous confidentiality, prompt, and non-committal regarding causes until forensic audits conclude.

### Do and Don't Rules
- DO acknowledge receipt immediately, confirm high-priority escalation to the Data Protection Officer (dpo@brightwavedigital.in), and supply incident ticket ID SEC-BWD-YYYY-XXXX.
- DO state that privacy rights requests (data erasure, rectification) are executed within 30 days under Section 12 of DPDP.
- DO assure the client that our security engineering unit has begun containment verification.
- DON'T speculate on vulnerabilities, acknowledge breaches, or admit fault before forensic verification.
- DON'T share logs, customer lists, or internal architecture details in unencrypted correspondence.

### Approved Reply Template
"Hello {{client_name}}, We take data privacy, GDPR compliance, and security with paramount urgency. We have logged your request under ticket SEC-BWD-2026-0891 and escalated it directly to our Data Protection Officer and Lead Security Engineer. For data deletion requests, our compliance team processes record scrubbing within 30 days pursuant to DPDP guidelines. If this pertains to a security concern, our team has initiated verification protocols. Our Head of Legal and Compliance will contact your designated security representative directly with an official status briefing."
