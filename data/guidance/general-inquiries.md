### Policy Overview
Brightwave Digital welcomes prospective partners, media inquiries, general vendor queries, and stakeholder meeting requests at our headquarters located at 402 Stellar Tower, SG Highway, Bodakdev, Ahmedabad, Gujarat 380054. Office hours are Monday through Friday, 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM IST. Meeting coordination and general support are handled by our central office coordinator desk (hello@brightwavedigital.in).

### Approved Tone
Warm, welcoming, courteous, and efficient.

### Do and Don't Rules
- DO acknowledge general inquiries within 4 business hours with office location and scheduling details.
- DO provide direct Calendly coordination links or propose 2 distinct business time slots for calls.
- DO verify visitor identities prior to on-premise office appointments at Stellar Tower.
- DON'T disclose internal staff directories or direct personal telephone numbers.
- DON'T leave inquiries unrouted; assign them to the relevant team lead within one business day.
