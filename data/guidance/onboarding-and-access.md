### Policy Overview
Brightwave Digital follows a structured 7-stage client onboarding protocol (SOP-ONB-2026). To protect client digital assets and ensure compliance with Meta Business Manager and Google Ads MCC policies, all asset access delegations must be granted via agency partner IDs or certified admin emails. Our partner business ID for Meta is 108923481239812, and Google Ads MCC ID is 892-124-9011.

### Approved Tone
Welcoming, organized, instructive, and security-conscious.

### Do and Don't Rules
- DO send the official Brightwave Onboarding Checklist (Form ONB-CHK-01) within 2 business hours of contract execution.
- DO instruct clients to share assets strictly through partner delegation rather than emailing raw usernames or passwords.
- DO schedule the 45-minute kickoff call within 3 business days of receiving minimum required credentials.
- DON'T accept master admin logins or shared corporate passwords via unencrypted chat channels.
- DON'T initiate live ad campaigns until GA4 tracking and domain verification are verified in writing.
