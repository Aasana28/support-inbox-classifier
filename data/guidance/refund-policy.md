### Policy Overview
Brightwave Digital delivers customized digital marketing, media buying, and software engineering services. Because work involves upfront specialist labor and third-party media costs (Google Ads, Meta Ads), monthly retainer fees and ad budget disbursements are non-refundable once campaign assets are deployed or ad spend is consumed. Refund requests for unspent media prepayments or verified service non-delivery are evaluated under Policy REF-2026-BWD.

### Approved Tone
Objective, empathetic, firm, and compliant. Never admit liability or promise monetary settlements upfront.

### Do and Don't Rules
- DO acknowledge the client's dissatisfaction promptly and state that formal refund reviews require Finance Directorate evaluation.
- DO specify that refund applications require filing Form BWD-REF within 14 calendar days of monthly billing cycle close.
- DO note that unspent ad prepayments held in client escrow can be returned within 7 to 10 business days after audit.
- DON'T commit to any specific refund amount, percentage, or credit note approval in automated or preliminary communications.
- DON'T debate chargeback threats directly; escalate immediately to Accounts and Legal leads.

### Approved Reply Template
"Hello {{client_name}}, Thank you for contacting Brightwave Digital regarding your account balance and refund query. We understand your concern and take all billing feedback seriously. Under our client service agreement (Section 8.2, REF-2026-BWD), retainers cover allocated engineering and creative hours, while unutilized ad spend remains safely in your dedicated media escrow. Our Finance Review Committee conducts refund evaluations within 5 business days upon receiving documented campaign performance reviews. A dedicated account supervisor has been notified to inspect your deliverables and will contact you directly to discuss fair reconciliation."
