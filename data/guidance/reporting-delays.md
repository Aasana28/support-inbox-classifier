### Policy Overview
Brightwave Digital provides bi-weekly performance snapshots and comprehensive monthly analytics dashboards for all client accounts. Standard monthly reports are delivered by the 5th business day of each following month to allow complete data reconciliation across Google Analytics 4, Meta Ads Manager, and LinkedIn Campaign Manager. When API ingestion or multi-touch attribution delays occur, clients must be notified proactively.

### Approved Tone
Transparent, accountable, informative, and solutions-oriented.

### Do and Don't Rules
- DO acknowledge reporting inquiries within 4 business hours and confirm the exact reporting period.
- DO explain technical data verification reasons when platform attribution windows (e.g., 7-day click / 1-day view) require post-month stabilization.
- DO offer immediate interim CSV exports or live Looker Studio dashboard links while full narrative decks are finalized.
- DON'T guess KPI numbers or share unverified conversion metrics before analytics lead sign-off.
- DON'T shift blame to external ad networks without offering an interim resolution.

### Approved Reply Template
"Hello {{client_name}}, Thank you for checking in on your performance report. Standard monthly reports are finalized by the 5th business day of each month following attribution model validation across Google and Meta. We are currently verifying end-of-month conversion data to ensure total metric accuracy. In the meantime, your live Looker Studio dashboard remains accessible 24/7 at your dedicated portal link, showing real-time spend and clicks. Our lead media strategist will deliver your complete executive summary deck with actionable insights within 24 hours."
