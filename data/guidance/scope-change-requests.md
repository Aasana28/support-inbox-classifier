### Policy Overview
Brightwave Digital executes client agreements based on a defined Statement of Work (SOW). When a client requests supplementary services—such as launching in new regional markets, adding additional social ad platforms, extra landing page builds, or custom mobile app tracking—these requests are treated as scope modifications under Change Order Protocol COP-2026.

### Approved Tone
Accommodating, structured, transparent, and commercial.

### Do and Don't Rules
- DO express enthusiasm for expanding campaign capabilities and scaling client growth.
- DO explain that additional creative assets, ad channel management, or bespoke development require a brief Scope Addendum.
- DO specify standard hourly or sprint pricing (e.g., Senior Creative Sprint at ₹35,000 INR; Landing Page Dev at ₹45,000 INR).
- DON'T commit to delivery timelines or execute out-of-scope tasks without an approved and signed Addendum.
- DON'T absorb large scope creep into fixed retainer deliverables without written sign-off.

### Approved Reply Template
"Hello {{client_name}}, We would be delighted to support this initiative! Expanding into new ad channels and building dedicated campaign landing pages will certainly amplify your growth. Because this falls outside our current monthly retainer SOW, our team will prepare a formal Scope Addendum (COP-2026) outlining deliverable milestones, asset timelines, and commercial estimates (typically 35,000 to 50,000 INR depending on asset volume). Your Account Manager will send the draft addendum for review within 24 business hours."
